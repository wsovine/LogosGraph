## Catalog issues (existing graph data the Catechism contradicts)

- §60 ['the-synagogue-as-preparation-for-the-church', 'deborah']: `the-synagogue-as-preparation-for-the-church` is labeled NT, but its name describes an OT reality (the preparation), not a New Testament fulfillment, and its only link is ← deborah, so the edge reads OT → OT. §60 states the preparation in the Catechism's own terms: the chosen people were 'called to prepare for that day when God would gather all his children into the unity of the Church' (Israel → the Church). Consider retiring this node and re-pointing deborah's link to the-one-true-church, or folding the idea into an OT Israel Type.
- §1222 ['jordan-crossing', 'eternal-life', 'the-promised-land']: The catalog links jordan-crossing → eternal-life and credits CCC 1222, but in §1222 'an image of eternal life' is in apposition to 'the gift of the land promised to Abraham's descendants': the promised land is the image of eternal life, and the Jordan crossing prefigures Baptism. The supported pairs are jordan-crossing → baptism and the-promised-land → eternal-life.
- §1333 ['melchizedek', 'eucharist']: The catalog credits CCC 1334 for Melchizedek, but §1334 never mentions him. The Melchizedek → Eucharist statement is §1333 ('a prefiguring of her own offering'), and §1350 repeats it ('takes up the gesture of Melchizedek').
